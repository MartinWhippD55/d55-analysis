# AI-DLC Workshop Offering — Critique Prompt

Use this prompt to critique the AI-DLC workshop assets from two perspectives. Provide the assets as context (dimensions.md, workshop.html, generate_spreadsheets.py, context.md) and then paste this prompt.

---

## The Prompt

I'd like you to critique the AI-DLC workshop offering assets I've provided from two distinct perspectives. Be direct, constructive, and specific. For each perspective, identify what's strong, what's missing, and what you'd change. Don't be polite — be useful.

---

### Perspective 1: CTO / Technical Director

You are the CTO of D55, a cloud consultancy that delivers data platforms, MLOps, and AI-enabled software. You're evaluating whether these assets are a credible starting point for a repeatable service offering. Your concerns are:

**Credibility & Depth**
- Do the 8 dimensions cover the right ground? Is anything missing that a technically sophisticated buyer would expect?
- Are the maturity rubrics defensible? Could a CTO at a prospect challenge the scoring and find it thin?
- Are the workshop questions sharp enough to surface real insight in 1 hour, or are they too generic / too many?
- Does the AI-First Delivery Lifecycle framing hold up technically? Would an experienced engineering leader buy it?

**Client Engagement**
- Would you feel confident handing these to a senior consultant and saying "run this workshop next week"? What's missing to make that possible?
- Does the gap-to-service mapping feel natural, or forced? Would a client feel sold to rather than helped?
- Is the 8-week prove-it model specific enough to scope and price, or is it still too hand-wavy?
- Are the qualifying questions strong enough to filter out bad-fit prospects before we invest the free hour?

**Technical Direction**
- Does this help us have an opinionated conversation about the RIGHT technical direction for a customer, or is it too agnostic/generic?
- Where would you want to see more AWS-specific depth vs platform-agnostic guidance?
- Are there obvious technical blind spots (security, networking, migration, integration with legacy systems)?

**Overall**
- On a scale of 1–5, how ready is this to use with a real prospect? What's the minimum viable improvement to get it there?
- What would make you proud to put the D55 name on this?

---

### Perspective 2: Marketing Director

You are the Marketing Director at D55. You need to create awareness, generate leads, and fill the top of the funnel for this new offering. You're evaluating whether these assets give you enough to work with. Your concerns are:

**Positioning & Messaging**
- Is the value proposition clear enough to communicate in a LinkedIn post, a landing page headline, or a 30-second pitch?
- What's the one-liner for this offering? Can you extract it from what's here, or is it buried?
- Does "AI-DLC" as a name work? Is it memorable, searchable, and does it communicate value to a non-technical buyer?
- How does this differentiate from the dozens of "AI maturity assessment" offerings already in market?

**Content & Collateral**
- Is there enough here to create: a landing page, a 1-pager PDF, a LinkedIn carousel, an email nurture sequence, a webinar outline?
- What specific claims, stats, or proof points could you pull out for marketing copy? Are they strong enough?
- The radar chart concept — is that visually compelling enough to be the hero image/hook in marketing materials?
- Is the "free assessment" framing the right commercial hook, or does it cheapen the offering?

**Target Audience & Funnel**
- Is the Ideal Customer Profile specific enough to target? Could you build an ad audience or an ABM list from it?
- What job titles should we target? Is that clear from the assets?
- What's the call-to-action? Is the conversion path obvious (see content → book assessment → attend → buy services)?
- Are there enough proof points / case studies / social proof to build trust, or is this all theoretical?

**Gaps**
- What would you need from the CTO/delivery team that isn't here yet to build a proper go-to-market?
- Is there a content calendar hiding in here? What topics could you extract for thought leadership?
- How would you price the "free" part in the buyer's mind — does it feel valuable enough to be worth an hour of a CTO's time?

**Overall**
- On a scale of 1–5, how ready is this from a marketing standpoint? What do you need to launch?
- What's the first marketing asset you'd build from this, and what's stopping you from building it today?

## How to Use This

1. Provide all the AI-DLC assets as context
2. Paste this prompt
3. Ask for the critique in a single structured response
4. Use the output to create a prioritised improvement backlog

The goal is to identify the gaps between "internal working document" and "client-ready service offering" — from both a technical credibility and commercial viability standpoint.
