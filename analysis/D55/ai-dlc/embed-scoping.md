# Phase 2 — Embed Scoping (One-Pager)

*The tool for turning the embed from a rate into a quotable engagement. Use this to scope and price a Phase 2 "Prove-It" embed after the assessment. Figures marked (illustrative) are working defaults pending Rhys sign-off — see `working-assumptions.md`.*

---

## What Phase 2 is

A deliberately **hybrid** squad — D55 engineers + the client's own people — ships live work together for a fixed proof cycle, then D55 tapers out as the client's people take over. Metrics baselined from day one. The output is shipped work, a proven ROI delta, and capability transferred into the client's team.

**Commercial model (per WA-2):** the embed is the **committed core** of the programme. Free assess and per-module teach are the stage-gated entry; committing to the embed is where the client commits to a programme price.

---

## Squad shape

| Role | D55 | Client | Notes |
|------|-----|--------|-------|
| AI-native engineers | 2–3 | 2–3 | The core pairing surface. 1:1 D55:client is the target ratio (illustrative). |
| Tech lead / orchestrator | 1 (D55, tapering) | → 1 (client, ramping) | Leadership deliberately transfers from D55 to client over the engagement. |
| Product owner / spec owner | — | 1 | Client-side; the middle-layer role Module 2/4 redefined. Essential — the embed stalls without it. |

**Target ratio:** ~1:1 D55-to-client engineers (illustrative). Below ~1 client person per 2 D55 is a red flag — it becomes staff-aug, not capability transfer.

**Minimum viable squad:** 2 D55 + 2 client + 1 client PO. Smaller than this and there aren't enough client people to transfer capability to.

## Duration model

**8-Week Prove-It (the committed proof cycle):**

| Weeks | Phase | What happens |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| 1–2 | Assess | Baseline metrics, wire up tooling, pick the first real work, establish the adapted lifecycle in the squad. |
| 3–6 | Prove | Ship real work as a hybrid pair-first team. Track the metric delta live. |
| 7–8 | Translate | Demonstrate results with numbers, capture playbooks into the manual, produce the rollout plan. |

**Then: taper (scoped per client).** The 8 weeks is the committed proof; a full embed usually continues for a taper period as D55 presence steps down. Quote as **"8-week proof, then taper."**

**Illustrative taper:** full D55 squad through week 8 → ~50% weeks 9–12 → ~25% weeks 13–16 → advisory/on-call thereafter. Shape it to the client's ability to absorb.

## Exit / capability-transfer criteria

D55 rolls off when these are true (not on a fixed date — on evidence):

- [ ] The metrics scorecard (from Module 1) shows a sustained delta the client can defend to their board.
- [ ] Client engineers are shipping AI-native work **without D55 pairing** on the same cadence.
- [ ] The client tech lead is running the adapted lifecycle (specs, ceremonies, PR review) unaided.
- [ ] Sections 2–5 of the Client Operating Manual are populated and the client's people can point to them and use them.
- [ ] A rollout plan for the next teams exists and has a client owner.

**Capability transfer is a measured deliverable, not a side effect** (value principle 2). If these aren't met, the taper extends — it doesn't get skipped.

## Pre-embed checklist (required inputs)

The embed depends on outputs from Modules 1, 3 and 4. If a module wasn't run as a full workshop (the gaps didn't call for it), its output is still **required** — captured as a compressed working session before the embed starts:

- [ ] **Metrics scorecard** (Module 1) — what we baseline and prove against. *Without this the embed can't prove ROI.*
- [ ] **Shipping guardrails** (Module 3) — AI usage policy, test strategy, governance controls. *Without these, shipped work can't safely reach production.*
- [ ] **Hybrid operating model** (Module 4) — squad structure, role split, handover plan. *This is the embed's team design.*

## Pricing (per WA-2)

- **Rate basis:** ~£15–25k / D55 engineer / month (existing figure).
- **Committed proof cycle:** the 8 weeks (2 months) of D55 squad cost is the committed programme spend the client signs up to.
- **Taper:** scoped and quoted separately as a ramp-down schedule, so the client sees the presence (and cost) stepping down as their capability ramps up.
- **Worked illustration (illustrative only):** 3 D55 engineers × 2 months committed proof ≈ £90–150k committed, plus a tapering follow-on scoped to the client. *Replace with real figures per engagement.*

## How to use this

1. Take the assessment radar + which modules ran.
2. Confirm the pre-embed checklist inputs exist (or scope compressed sessions to create them).
3. Size the squad from the client's available people (aim ~1:1) and the scope of the first real work.
4. Fix the 8-week proof cycle; sketch a taper to the client's absorption rate.
5. Set the exit criteria with the client up front — they define "done."
6. Price: committed proof cycle + scoped taper.
