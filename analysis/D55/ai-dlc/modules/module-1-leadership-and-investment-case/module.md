# Module 1 — Leadership & the Investment Case

## Objective
Turn AI-assisted development from a grassroots experiment into a sponsored, measured business initiative. Leaders leave with a mandate, a named sponsor, an investment case they can defend to the board/PE, and the scorecard we'll prove it against.

## Why it matters (client outcome)
Without executive mandate and a measurement frame, AI adoption stalls at "some devs use Copilot" and can never be scaled or defended when budgets tighten. This module makes the case and sets the yardstick.

## Who's in the room
Exec sponsor (essential), CTO/VP Eng, CFO or finance partner, and PE/board representation where relevant. Small, senior, decision-making.

## Inputs (from assessment)
- Current vs target scores on Leadership & Mandate and Metrics & ROI
- Intake-form context: who's driving AI, budget status, board/PE pressure
- Any existing metrics (or the absence of them)

## Session flow
1. **Where you are** — replay the assessment: mandate and measurement scores, what they imply.
2. **The economics** — the £200/seat for 3–5x argument, sized to their engineer count.
3. **The real cost is delay** — reframe from "cost of tooling" to "cost of not moving."
4. **Define the mandate** — optional vs encouraged vs mandated; name the sponsor; agree the organisational position.
5. **Build the scorecard** — agree the handful of metrics we'll baseline and prove against (cycle time, deployment frequency, throughput, defect rate, output per £).
6. **The path** — how the 8-Week Prove-It gives them hard numbers to take to the board.

## Deliverables (what they leave with)
- A one-page investment case (sized to their org)
- Named executive sponsor and agreed mandate position
- An agreed metrics scorecard for the programme
- Board/PE-ready framing of the ROI argument

## Writes to Client Operating Manual
Section 1 — Mandate & Measurement: the sponsor, the mandate position, the metrics scorecard, and the investment case baseline.

## How it sets up the embed
The scorecard defined here is exactly what Phase 2 baselines from day one. No scorecard, no proof — so this module de-risks the embed's ability to demonstrate ROI.
