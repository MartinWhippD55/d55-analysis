# Module 4 — People & Change

## Objective
Address the human side of AI adoption — the part that actually determines whether it lands or stalls. Diagnose resistance, redefine coordination roles, upskill, and define how to structure and run a hybrid AI-native team (the operating model Phase 2 embodies and hands over).

## Why it matters (client outcome)
Technology maturity doesn't predict success — people adaptation does. Resistance is personality-driven, not stupidity, and it needs diagnosing not steamrolling. And when devs go faster, coordination roles become the bottleneck. This module also answers the question the embed raises: "what does our team look like, and how do we run it, once D55 leaves?"

## Who's in the room
Leadership and eng managers, POs/BAs/PMs (the middle layer), and HR/People. Broad by design — change is cross-functional.

## Inputs (from assessment)
- Current vs target on Team Adaptation & Skills
- Intake context: where resistance is coming from, champions vs blockers, whether the middle layer is keeping pace
- Whether the client intends a hybrid embed (feeds the operating-model section)

## Session flow
1. **The Four Fears** — run the diagnostic: Controller, Driver, Stabiliser, Influencer. Which fears, which people?
2. **The reframes** — how AI addresses each fear when positioned right (more oversight, compressed risk, orchestrator value, amplification).
3. **The middle-layer bottleneck** — name it and plan the role shift (POs as spec owners, BAs as requirements-prompt engineers).
4. **Upskilling** — what a real AI-first training/onboarding path looks like.
5. **The hybrid team operating model** — how D55 + their people work as one squad, how work splits, and how the handover happens so they're self-sufficient after we leave.
6. **Change plan** — tailored interventions per resistance pattern.

## Deliverables (what they leave with)
- A Four Fears diagnostic of their org (who, what fear, what reframe)
- A middle-layer role-shift plan
- An upskilling / onboarding path
- A hybrid team operating model and handover plan

## Writes to Client Operating Manual
Section 4 — People & Operating Model: the resistance diagnosis and interventions, redefined roles, the upskilling path, and the hybrid-team operating model + handover plan.

## How it sets up the embed
This is the module most tightly coupled to Phase 2. The hybrid operating model defined here *is* how the embed team is structured, and the handover plan is what makes D55's exit real rather than the client falling back to old habits. Do this module and the embed has a shape to step into.
