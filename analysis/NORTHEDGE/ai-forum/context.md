# Northedge AI Forum

## Background

Northedge are the PE house backing Cezanne. They're unhappy with pace and cost at Cezanne and have moved the CTO out. There's a gap for us to fill if we can demonstrate we know what we're doing.

**Our angle:** We can help them adopt AI to become more productive/smaller.

This questionnaire forms the agenda for a meeting with Cezanne next week.

## Goal

To understand what separates organisations generating real, scalable value from AI from those in experimentation — particularly how they align AI to business outcomes, build trust, drive adoption, manage emerging risks, and develop the operating models and talent needed to succeed.

Identify the practical patterns, governance approaches, and product & engineering practices that enable measurable impact while avoiding common pitfalls at scale.
