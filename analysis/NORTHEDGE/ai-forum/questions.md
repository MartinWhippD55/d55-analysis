# AI Forum — Questions & Agenda

## 1. Aligning AI to Business Outcomes

How are leading organisations aligning AI to create tangible business outcomes? What are the most common ways this goes wrong despite significant investment? We are keen to learn from failure stories to avoid common pitfalls.

## 2. Building Customer Confidence & Trust

What have you seen work to build customer confidence and trust in AI that drives adoption? We are keen to understand common resistance to AI trust and adoption, and how other, particularly regulated, businesses have overcome this challenge.

### 2.1. Embedding Governance, Risk & Trust Without Slowing Delivery

- Practical application of governance (incl. EU AI Act readiness) at model, feature, and product level
- Ensuring accuracy, transparency, and oversight (bias, hallucination, drift, data leakage)
- Defining when AI is assistive vs advisory vs decision-making
- Ownership and operating model for governance across product, engineering, and compliance

## 4. What Separates Real Value from Experimentation?

What separates organisations that are generating real, scalable value from AI from those in experimentation? What patterns consistently appear on both sides? We are keen to learn from real world examples and discover repeatable patterns we can use.

## 5. Competitive Race or Structural Shift?

Are we in a true competitive race with AI, or is this a slower structural shift? How should that change the pace and type of investment organisations make today?

- How other leaders are prioritising and sequencing AI decisions
- How should we be thinking about the long-term costs of AI across the business

## 6. What Does "Good" Look Like at Scale?

What does "good" look like when AI is truly embedded at scale, in terms of operating model, talent, and culture? What are the key inflection points to move from experimentation to that state?

### 6.1. High-Performing AI-Enabled Product Organisation

- Roles, skills, and operating model (central vs embedded AI capability)
- Moving from experimentation to repeatable, scalable AI capability
- Where AI creates the biggest productivity gains vs common failure points

### 6.2. AI-First Product Development Lifecycle

- Where AI should be embedded across discovery, design and delivery
- How to standardise prompts, patterns, and outputs for consistency
- Balancing creativity vs quality, and avoiding low-value AI noise
- Integrating AI into core tooling (e.g. Figma, Jira, dev pipelines)

### 6.3. Preventing Product Becoming a Bottleneck

- Where bottlenecks typically emerge in AI-enabled teams
- How AI can reduce dependency on product managers and accelerate decisions
- Operating models and guardrails that enable speed without fragmentation
- Minimising review/approval overhead while maintaining standards

### 6.4. AI Architecture & Operating Model

- What design patterns are emerging as best practice
- Key architectural mistakes and how to avoid them
- Where are people over-engineering vs under-engineering
- What are the most common architecture mistakes teams make with agentic systems?
- Human-in-the-loop design: where it adds value vs slows things down
- Should we consider open-source alternatives to LangSmith to reduce costs
- Which text-to-speech models are you seeing be most effective
- Fine-tuning vs prompt engineering, structured outputs, or a tools/RAG layer

### 6.5. Evaluation, Guardrails & Governance at Scale

- How leading teams implement evals (offline + online) and governance
- Making evals reliable enough to gate CI (reducing variance, increasing determinism)
- Guardrails, safety, and compliance patterns in production systems
- Maintaining and updating fine-tuned models in regulated domains

### 6.6. Memory, Personalisation & Context Management

- Best patterns for user-level memory (e.g. vector store + summarisation, structured profile + RAG, or a hybrid)
- Separating and managing user vs organisation-level context safely
- Strategies for memory lifecycle management (decay, expiry, re-evaluation)
- Ensuring personalisation improves relevance without introducing risk or drift

### 6.7. Measuring Real AI Impact

- Metrics that meaningfully capture value (quality, outcomes, efficiency, not just usage)
- Identifying where teams are mis-measuring or over-optimising
- Linking AI investments to tangible product and customer impact

## 7. Source of Competitive Advantage

Where is the real source of competitive advantage now — access to AI tools, proprietary data, or having AI-native teams? How might that change over the next few years?

- Priority use cases for differentiation (e.g. HR/payroll, compliance-heavy domains)
- Balancing AI innovation vs core roadmap delivery
- Ensuring AI delivers measurable customer value (not novelty)
- Key trade-offs, risks, and what failure would look like if executed poorly
