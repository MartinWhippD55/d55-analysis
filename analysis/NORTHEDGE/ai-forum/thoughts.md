## Four Fears Framework

The four personality types each have a distinct primary fear when it comes to AI adoption. But if you shift perspective, AI also reinforces or mitigates those fears.

### The Matrix

|                        | Past/Present Leaning                        | Future Thinking                |
|------------------------|---------------------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| **Tasks-oriented**     | Loss of Control (blue/Controller)           | Project Failure (red/Driver)   |
| **People-oriented**    | Disruption to Org Structure (green/Stabiliser) | Feeling Overshadowed (yellow/Influencer) |

### The Fears

- **Blue (Controller)** — "I can't manage what I can't understand. AI makes things unpredictable."
- **Red (Driver)** — "This could go wrong and I'll be accountable. The risk is too high."
- **Green (Stabiliser)** — "My position in the org, my relationships, my authority — all under threat."
- **Yellow (Influencer)** — "If AI does the impressive stuff, what's my value? Where do I shine?"

### The Flip — AI as the Answer to Each Fear

- **Blue → More control, not less.** AI gives you better observability, metrics, and consistency. Control shifts from manual gatekeeping to systemic oversight.
- **Red → Faster failure recovery, not more failure.** AI compresses feedback loops. You fail faster, cheaper, and smaller. The real project risk is slow delivery and late discovery — AI reduces that.
- **Green → Hierarchy reshapes, doesn't disappear.** People who learn to orchestrate AI become more valuable. Their org position strengthens if they lean in. The threat is only real if they stand still.
- **Yellow → Amplification, not replacement.** AI handles the grunt work and frees space for creative, strategic, interpersonal work. More stage, not less.

### Application to Cezanne

- The CTO who got moved out — which quadrant was he stuck in?
- Resistance from POs/BAs/PMs — primarily green and blue (hierarchy disruption + loss of control)
- Northedge themselves — likely red-leaning (PE mindset, risk-aware, outcome-focused)
- This framework gives Cezanne's leadership a lens to diagnose their own blockers and tailor approach per person/role
